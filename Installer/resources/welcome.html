Welcome to Credo __VERSION__

Credo shows your Claude token usage in the menu bar and in desktop widgets.

On the next step you can choose:

  • Remove previous versions first: on by default. Removes older Credo or TokenMeter apps for a clean install.
  • Also erase saved history and settings: off by default. Start completely fresh.
  • Install Credo: untick it to uninstall only.

Credo installs to /Applications and opens when the installer finishes.
